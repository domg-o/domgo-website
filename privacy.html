Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Who we are

Domgo is the creator portfolio website of Dominic George-Oppong, trading as JDB Brothers (“we”, “us” or “our”).

We are the data controller for personal information processed through this website.

  • Website: https://domgo.co.uk
  • Email: dom@domgo.co.uk

2. Information we collect

We may collect or receive:

  • Contact information and the contents of messages you send to us by email.
  • Basic technical information generated when you visit the site, such as IP address, browser and device information, referral source and pages viewed, where supplied by our hosting or security providers.
  • If you choose to connect an Instagram or TikTok account through an official authorisation flow, the limited account data and access credentials needed to provide the feature. This may include account identifiers, public profile information, follower statistics and public post metadata. We do not receive or store your social media password.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Reply to brand, partnership and media kit enquiries.
  • Operate, secure and improve the website.
  • Display approved public social media figures and selected public post links when the social media integration is enabled.
  • Meet legal obligations and protect our rights.

We do not sell personal information or use it for automated decision-making.

4. Our legal bases

Depending on the activity, we rely on:

  • Legitimate interests to run a secure, reliable portfolio site and respond to genuine business enquiries.
  • Steps taken at your request where you contact us about a potential collaboration.
  • Consent and/or legitimate interests when an account owner chooses to connect a social media account for the purpose described during the authorisation flow.

5. Cookies and analytics

The site does not currently use cookies, analytics or other tracking technologies.

If we add optional analytics in future, it will stay switched off unless you opt in, and we will update this policy before it goes live.

6. Who we share information with

We share personal information only with the providers that help us run the site, and only as far as they need it.

  • GitHub hosts the website through GitHub Pages and may log technical information, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.
  • ImprovMX, Google (Gmail) and Brevo handle our email. ImprovMX forwards messages sent to dom@domgo.co.uk, Gmail stores them and Brevo sends our replies.
  • TikTok and Meta (Instagram) supply account data through their official APIs when the account owner connects an account. Their own privacy policies apply to the data they hold.
  • Our server provider runs the secure service that stores social media access credentials and refreshes the figures shown on the site.
  • Professional advisers, regulators or law enforcement where the law requires it or where we need to protect our rights.

7. International transfers

Some of these providers process information outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is protected by a safeguard recognised under UK data protection law, such as UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

8. How long we keep information

  • Enquiry emails are kept for up to 2 years after our last contact. Where a collaboration goes ahead, we keep the related records for up to 6 years to meet tax and accounting obligations.
  • Social media access credentials are kept only while the connection is active. We delete them within 30 days of the account owner disconnecting or revoking access.
  • Public figures and post links shown on the site are replaced each time the data refreshes.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your information.
  • Ask us to restrict how we use your information.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Receive your information in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that took place before.

Email dom@domgo.co.uk to use any of these rights. There is no charge, and we will reply within one month.

An account owner who connected a TikTok or Instagram account can also revoke access at any time from that platform’s app permission settings.

10. Complaints

Please contact us first so we can try to resolve your concern. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

11. Security

The whole site runs over HTTPS. Social media access credentials are stored on the server only and are never sent to visitors’ browsers. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

12. Children

This website is intended for brands, partners and general visitors. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

13. Other websites

The site links to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Snapchat and other third-party websites. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always appear on this page with its “Last updated” date.

15. Contact

For any question about this policy, email dom@domgo.co.uk.
